# Q9 · Where are PE-associated genes normally expressed?

**Working hypothesis.** The villous PE signature may combine angiogenic or endocrine genes enriched in trophoblast or vascular classes with metabolic and stress genes expressed across several lineages. Ask whether the bulk signature is more concentrated than equally detectable genes. Baseline localization depends on stage, tissue, and assay. Control samples from Admati provide a delivery-stage reference, and healthy atlases add developmental and spatial context. [Admati et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37572658/) · [GSE198373](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE198373) · [Wang et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10316-x)

Build two figures for the bulk signature and its robustness, then the control-expression heatmap with signed bulk effects and the matched-null comparison. Label row scaling and show donor coverage.
