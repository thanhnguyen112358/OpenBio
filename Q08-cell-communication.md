# Q8 · Do trophoblast and endothelial RNA patterns suggest altered communication?

**Working hypothesis.** Early PE may show higher `FLT1` and lower `PGF` RNA in syncytiotrophoblast (SCT), with a compatible response in placental endothelium from the same donors. Soluble sFLT1 can sequester VEGF and PlGF. The dataset measures gene-level RNA, so the added evidence here is whether sender and receiver profiles are consistent with that model at delivery. A secondary, direction-agnostic late-PE hypothesis pairs villous cytotrophoblast `ANXA1` with macrophage `FPR2`. [Maynard et al.](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/17189) · [Admati et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37572658/) · [Lee et al., preprint](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41472684/)

## Analysis

1. Use Admati donor-by-class profiles and a proposed ≥20-cell threshold. All 26 donors meet the threshold for SCT and endothelium, and the planning chat found detectable SCT `FLT1`, `PGF`, `VEGFA`, and endothelial `KDR` in each. Join on `pseudobulk_id` and inspect class coverage, count depth, and PCA. The primary comparison is 10 early PE versus 3 early controls. Keep 7 late PE versus 6 late controls as a separate contrast. [Profiles](openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/data/extension/pseudobulk/track01_pseudobulk_metadata.csv) · [Donors](openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/data/extension/pseudobulk/track01_pseudobulk_donors.csv)
2. Prespecify separate endothelial angiogenic and inflammatory readouts. Fit one raw-count model per class and report log₂ effects, intervals, donor values, and multiple-testing correction. `KDR` RNA gives receptor expression context. Signaling could still change through ligand availability at stable `KDR` expression. A direct early-minus-late contrast is required for an onset-specific association.
3. Plot expression against delivery age and indicate FGR and delivery context. Leave out each early control in turn, inspect the overlap subset where feasible, and show the four early cases without FGR separately. Align donor order across SCT `FLT1`/`PGF`, endothelial `KDR`, and receiver-response panels. Examine within-group patterns before interpreting pooled correlation.
4. Record at least three browser lookups, starting with `FLT1`, `PGF`, and `KDR`. Note which finer cell labels express each gene. Use GSE203507 villous bulk as a separate tissue-level direction check. The extension samples placental cotyledons cleared of decidua. The measured endothelial population is local placental endothelium. The healthy atlas’s decidual NK `CCL5`–EVT `CCR1` hypothesis belongs in anatomical context, given sparse EVT controls and the tissue sampled. [Browser guide](openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/data/interpretation/zeisel_browser_guide.md) · [Vento-Tormo et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-018-0698-6)
5. If the targeted analysis warrants expansion, test late `ANXA1`, `FPR2`, and a fixed macrophage response signature. Both genes were detectable in their proposed classes across the late donors in the source chat. For discovery, prioritize detectable partners and required receptor subunits, plausible anatomy, and a compatible receiver response. Document the ligand–receptor resource version.

## Figures and follow-up

Use two figures for cohort coverage and aligned trophoblast–endothelial effects. A robust shift in both classes supports a candidate angiogenic mechanism, with shared placental stress as a competing explanation. A robust SCT shift with uncertain receiver effects supports a trophoblast association and leaves local communication unresolved. A follow-up could measure soluble `FLT1` isoforms, secreted sFLT1, and available VEGF/PlGF, then ask whether a trophoblast-derived signal changes placental endothelial VEGFR2 activity and whether restoring ligand availability reverses it.
