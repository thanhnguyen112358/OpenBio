# Presentation script

Use the article figures in the order below. Keep the recording under five minutes. Assign these sections so that every team member appears or speaks, and add the team byline to the opening or closing card.

### The comparison

When fetal growth is restricted, what changes in the placenta if the pregnancy also develops early preeclampsia? We looked at chorionic villi, the part of the placenta that supports exchange between mother and fetus. We focused on a family of signals called B M P, and compared pregnancies with both diagnoses with pregnancies that had growth restriction alone.

### Delivery age

We used RNA measurements at delivery from the OpenBio release of Ackerman's study and from a second study by Awamleh and colleagues. We assigned each pregnancy one RNA score based on twenty seven B M P related genes. The OpenBio groups were in the same inferred sequencing round, but they differed in delivery timing. This chart shows all twelve pregnancies. Only two of the seven combined cases delivered within the range covered by the five isolated cases. Even inside the shaded band, the two combined cases delivered earlier than most isolated cases.

### Pregnancy-level results

Each dot in the upper panels represents one pregnancy. The bars show group averages. The combined group averaged zero point two seven three log two score units higher in OpenBio, and zero point one four five higher in Awamleh. Both stayed positive after dropping any one pregnancy. Below, the long middle line shows that OpenBio's shared-age estimate is imprecise. Awamleh included all released samples, and we could only model diagnosis using the public sample metadata. Isolated growth restriction also had a higher score than preterm controls in Awamleh.

### Gene ranking

We also ranked genes by the strength and direction of their differences. B M P genes tended to appear toward the higher-expression end. The type two receptor B M P R two contributed in all three analyses, along with a second receptor and an antagonist. Removing two genes shared with the activin pathway weakened the score difference in both cohorts. Related signaling pathways shifted too, so the RNA pattern may be part of a broader response.

### Where the RNA appears

We used a placental reference to ask which cells express these genes. Larger dots mean a higher share of donors had at least ten reads. Darker dots show higher RNA levels. Two genes, B M P R two and SMAD nine, were strongest in cells lining blood vessels. The antagonist GREM two was strongest in the outer trophoblast layer. The reference had no isolated growth restriction comparison group. Changes in cell proportions could account for some of the difference seen in bulk tissue.

### What to measure next

These results describe RNA at delivery. Timing, growth restriction severity, labor, delivery mode and cell composition may all matter. A new cohort with comparable delivery ages could measure activated SMAD proteins inside specific cells and count those cell types in the same placental sections. If the higher RNA score accompanies a shift in cell proportions with similar signaling inside each cell type, that would weaken the proposed within-cell mechanism.
