# Analysis plan

## Research question

Among pregnancies with fetal growth restriction (FGR), how much higher or lower is the mean bone morphogenetic protein (BMP) RNA score in chorionic villi at delivery when early-onset preeclampsia (EOPE) is also present? The primary estimate subtracts the isolated-FGR mean from the EOPE+FGR mean within each cohort. A pilot that included OpenBio and Awamleh informed the choice of comparison and pathway, so estimates from both cohorts are exploratory.

## Datasets

| Dataset ID | Role | Why this dataset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OpenBio Track 1 / Ackerman, GSE203507](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE203507) | Primary comparison, 7 EOPE+FGR versus 5 isolated FGR. Paired decidua extension, 4 versus 5. | The release provides villous RNA counts, clinical groups, delivery weeks, fetal sex, and matched decidua for a subset. |
| [Awamleh, GSE114691](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE114691) | Separate cohort examined in the pilot, 20 EOPE+FGR versus 18 isolated FGR. | It provides the same diagnostic comparison plus EOPE-only and preterm control groups for context. |
| [Admati placental atlas, Figshare 23264102](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.23264102.v1) | Cell-class localization using OpenBio donor-class counts and the organizer's gene browser. | It identifies cell classes that can contribute the transcripts seen in bulk tissue. |
| [Piekos placental multiomics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-08631-6) | Severe-preeclampsia extension, 25 `FGR+PE Severe` versus 36 `FGR`. | It provides an additional contrast with delivery age available, using the released severity labels. |
| [MSigDB Reactome 2024.1.Hs](https://data.broadinstitute.org/gsea-msigdb/msigdb/release/2024.1.Hs/) | Gene sets for scoring and rank analysis. | The versioned definitions specify the BMP, activin, and TGF-β receptor branches. |

## Methods

1. **Load and audit the counts.** Check nonnegative integer counts, unique count and metadata IDs, and one library per OpenBio donor within each tissue. Reconcile OpenBio count columns, metadata, the donor audit, and HTSeq totals. Retain the organizer's clinical labels and converted delivery weeks. For Awamleh, match GEO accessions to diagnoses and aggregate transcript-feature counts to genes using Ensembl GRCh37 release 75. Use variance-stabilized principal components from the 500 most variable genes to inspect the OpenBio villous libraries by diagnosis, fetal sex, and inferred sequencing round.
2. **Compare the two FGR groups.** Both arms have FGR, which makes the comparison specific to the additional association with EOPE among pregnancies affected by FGR. Start with the 31 genes in `REACTOME_SIGNALING_BY_BMP` (R-HSA-201451). Keep genes with unique HGNC and Ensembl mappings and at least 10 raw reads in at least five libraries in each full villous cohort. Average log₂(normalized count + 1) for the 27 passing genes to obtain one score per pregnancy, using median-ratio scaling within each full cohort. Estimate group mean differences with approximate 95% Welch intervals. Keep the same genes and size factors in the shared-age, blood pressure flag, and leave-one-out checks. Repeat after removing INHA and INHBA to assess the contribution of the two genes shared with activin. Compare Awamleh EOPE-only with FGR and FGR with preterm controls for context.
3. **Fit count models and test rank enrichment.** Fit PyDESeq2 `~ fetal_sex + group` to all 30 OpenBio villous libraries and refit it to the seven pregnancies in the shared delivery-age range. Fit `~ group` to all 79 released Awamleh libraries. Report unshrunken log₂ fold changes, two-sided Wald tests, approximate 95% Wald intervals, and genome-wide false discovery rate (FDR) at 0.05. Rank by signed Wald statistic using genes with finite statistics and p-values, base mean ≥ 10, unambiguous symbols, and reads in at least one of the two arms. Intersect eligible symbols from five unrestricted contrasts per cohort and the restricted OpenBio contrast. Use GSEApy adaptive multilevel sampling with seed 1049, one thread, gene set sizes 10–500, and epsilon 10⁻¹⁰. Apply Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment across BMP, activin, and TGF-β receptor branches within each fit.
4. **Check the gene browser and atlas.** Record the cell classes with visible expression for BMPR2, GREM2, and SMAD9. Log the unavailable BMPR1B browser panel and use the released atlas counts for its expression context. For the shared leading-edge genes and the browser genes, summarize median counts per million and the fraction of donor-class libraries with at least 10 reads. Require at least 20 cells per donor-class and at least three eligible donors per class. [Gene browser](https://zeisellab.org/preeclampsia/) · [Lookup log](../notebooks/browser_lookups.csv)
5. **Run paired tissue and severe-preeclampsia extensions.** For paired OpenBio tissues, normalize villi and decidua separately and keep score genes that also meet the coverage threshold in decidua. Compare the two tissue scores and each donor's decidua-minus-villi score. In Piekos, estimate the group difference using the same 27-gene score, with unadjusted and shared-age models and HC3 intervals. Within shared age support, fit a linear delivery-week model. Check the audited suffix match against exact-ID linkage and repeat using the earlier 31-gene score with a +0.5 pseudocount.
6. **Assess marker and delivery-week adjustment.** Score OpenBio endothelial markers (PECAM1, VWF, CDH5, CLDN5), syncytiotrophoblast markers (CGA, ERVW-1, PSG1), and perivascular markers (RGS5, MCAM, PDGFRB, NOTCH3) using the same normalization. In the 7 versus 5 comparison, estimate the group coefficient by ordinary least squares with HC3 intervals. Compare the unadjusted model with models including the endothelial and syncytiotrophoblast marker scores, individually and together, delivery week alone and with both marker scores, and a perivascular sensitivity. These models were specified after the unadjusted score comparison. Report receptor, antagonist, and canonical SMAD summaries and examine selected genes individually. [Model estimates](../results/composition_effects.csv) · [Gene estimates](../results/composition_gene_effects.csv)
7. **Interpret the estimates together.** Compare the direction, magnitude, and uncertainty of the pregnancy scores. Use the rank results to show which genes contribute and the atlas to identify plausible source cells. The ranking and score reuse the same bulk libraries. A follow-up cohort can test whether the score difference persists after measuring cell fractions, whether BMPR2 RNA differs inside endothelial cells, and whether nuclear phosphorylated SMAD1/5/9 supports altered signaling. ## Confounders and controls

| Factor | How we address it |
| --- | --- |
| Delivery week | Report the full OpenBio estimate, a linear delivery-week adjustment, and restriction to the shared 26.714–30.429 week window. The restriction retains 2 EOPE+FGR and 5 isolated-FGR pregnancies, with residual age imbalance. The linear model uses observations outside the shared range. Awamleh's public matched metadata contain diagnosis but lack individual delivery weeks. |
| Fetal sex and sequencing round | Include fetal sex in the OpenBio gene count models. Verify that the primary OpenBio arms share an inferred sequencing round and inspect separation by round across all villous libraries. Awamleh lacks corresponding sample-level covariates in the matched release. |
| Clinical labels and sample selection | Keep the released labels and repeat the primary OpenBio score after omitting the two isolated-FGR pregnancies with blood pressure flags, separately and together. Use the full Awamleh release because the original authors' retained sample IDs could not be verified. |
| Cell composition and sampling site | Use one tissue for the primary comparison, analyze matched tissues separately, and estimate the association after adjusting for marker RNA. Marker RNA reflects cell abundance and expression within cells. The Admati atlas adds localization context and has no isolated-FGR group for the primary contrast. |
| FGR severity, labor, and mode of delivery | Record available metadata and carry these potential sources of confounding into interpretation. Report an association at delivery. |
