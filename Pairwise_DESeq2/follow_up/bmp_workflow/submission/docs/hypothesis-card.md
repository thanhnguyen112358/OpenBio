# Higher villous BMP-associated RNA when early preeclampsia accompanies growth restriction

**Clinical question.** Among pregnancies with fetal growth restriction, does early-onset preeclampsia accompany a higher villous BMP-associated RNA score at delivery?

**Observation.** The 27-gene score was +0.273 log₂ in OpenBio (7 versus 5; interval 0.100 to 0.446) and +0.145 in Awamleh (20 versus 18; 0.037 to 0.253). Leave-one-out kept both differences positive. A straight-line adjustment for OpenBio delivery week left +0.286 (0.068 to 0.503). The shared-week subset keeps two combined pregnancies, and that interval runs from −0.752 to +1.281. Removing INHA and INHBA, which the BMP list shares with activin, reduced Awamleh to +0.085 and the interval included zero. In Piekos the unadjusted severe-PE contrast was +0.174 (0.066 to 0.283, 25 versus 36); the age-adjusted estimate inside the shared weeks was +0.110 (−0.069 to 0.289). On nine paired OpenBio pregnancies the decidual difference was +0.415 (0.058 to 0.772) and the villous difference was +0.284 (−0.024 to 0.592).

Inside the OpenBio score, BMPR2 was +0.610 (0.054 to 1.167) and SMAD9 was +0.668 (0.148 to 1.189). BMPR1A was not higher. GREM2 averaged +0.838 with an interval that included zero. SMAD1 and SMAD5 were not higher. The endothelial marker score was +0.242 (0.032 to 0.452) and the syncytiotrophoblast marker score was +0.319 (−0.279 to 0.917). The 27-gene score correlated with those marker scores at r = 0.28 and r = 0.10. Adjusting for either or both left the BMP difference at +0.294 to +0.355, with wider intervals that include zero. SMAD9 tracked the endothelial score (r = 0.64) and its difference fell to +0.424 after that adjustment. BMPR2 did not (r = 0.15) and remained higher after the same adjustment. In the Admati reference, BMPR2 and SMAD9 are highest in endothelium and GREM2 is highest in syncytiotrophoblast. [Article and figures](article.md)

**Prediction.** In a new set of EOPE+FGR and isolated-FGR pregnancies with overlapping delivery ages, counted fractions of villous endothelium and syncytiotrophoblast will leave a higher 27-gene score in the combined group. BMPR2 RNA will be higher within endothelial cells after those fractions are fixed. SMAD9 and GREM2 are secondary targets. Nuclear pSMAD1/5/9 measured within those cell classes will test for a signaling difference, with the direction left open. If counted cell fractions account for the bulk score, that would weaken the within-cell explanation of the score. Yi et al. described altered BMP2 and its receptors in EOPE placenta. Deng et al. reported BMP2 signaling through SMAD2/3 in trophoblasts. [Yi et al.](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2021.768669) · [Deng et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2023.104664)

**Test.** Recruit independent EOPE+FGR and isolated-FGR pregnancies with overlap in delivery age. Record labor, delivery mode and FGR severity, and sample matched villous regions. Measure the 27-gene score, BMPR2, SMAD9 and GREM2, and on adjacent sections quantify endothelial and syncytiotrophoblast proportions together with nuclear pSMAD1/5/9. Include pSMAD2/3 because activin genes contribute to the score. Summarize each measurement per pregnancy. Estimate the score difference before and after the cell fractions, and estimate BMPR2 and pSMAD1/5/9 within endothelium.

**Confidence and relevance.** OpenBio and Awamleh agree on the direction, with additional support from the unadjusted severe-PE contrast in Piekos. The OpenBio marker-adjusted estimates are positive and imprecise. Delivery-age overlap is limited, activin genes contribute to the Awamleh association, and the paired tissues leave open whether the signal is stronger in either tissue. Marker RNA is an indirect measure of cell abundance. The useful next measurement is cell-resolved BMPR2 and nuclear pSMAD, with the cell fractions recorded on the same sections.
