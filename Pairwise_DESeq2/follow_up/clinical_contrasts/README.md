# Clinical contrast follow-up

See the [BMP workflow](../../../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-15-bmp-eope-fgr-workflow.md) for the fixed donor score, cohort checks and datathon deliverables.

The primary comparison is EOPE+FGR versus isolated FGR in villous tissue. Both groups have fetal growth restriction. The EOPE-only versus isolated FGR comparison shows how the pattern behaves in the other preeclampsia group. EOPE-only versus idiopathic preterm birth provides context from the earlier screen. The local combined and isolated FGR groups fall in the same inferred sequencing round. The full local villous comparison has 7 versus 5 pregnancies. Only 2 versus 5 fall within the shared delivery-age range (26.714–30.429 weeks). The age restriction leaves differences in age within that range.

The Hallmark TGF-β signaling set had positive normalized enrichment for EOPE+FGR versus isolated FGR in OpenBio (+2.19), in the age restriction (+1.57), and in the separate Awamleh cohort (+1.79). The corresponding mean log₂ expression scores per donor differed by +0.213 (approximate 95% Welch CI +0.063 to +0.363), +0.124 (−0.103 to +0.350), and +0.052 (−0.019 to +0.122), respectively. After removing ENG, the effects were +0.185, +0.098, and +0.036. The two restricted or external intervals still include zero. In Awamleh, isolated FGR itself was +0.069 versus preterm controls on the full pathway expression score. Gene set enrichment addresses the relative ranking of genes. The score and its interval describe the magnitude and spread across pregnancies.

The Reactome BMP signaling set had positive rank enrichment in the same three primary fits (+1.96, +1.61, +1.79 on the same 25 mapped members). The fixed donor BMP score differences were +0.273 (approximate 95% Welch CI +0.100 to +0.446), +0.264 (−0.752 to +1.281), and +0.145 (+0.037 to +0.253). The score difference was positive after each single-donor omission. In the small age subset two combined cases give very little precision. Isolated FGR also had a higher donor score than preterm controls in Awamleh (+0.146, +0.047 to +0.244). The direct contrast estimates an additional association with co-occurring PE in that cohort. Activin also had a positive donor contrast, so the screen does not isolate one signaling branch.

Seven genes contribute to positive enrichment of the Reactome BMP set in all three primary fits (BMPR1B, BMPR2, GREM2, INHA, INHBA, SMAD4, and SMAD9). BMPR2 is an example of a receptor in the BMP branch. Its unshrunken log₂ effects are +0.55 locally, +0.39 in the age restriction, and +0.29 in Awamleh. The age-restricted single-gene estimate has a wide Wald interval. Expression at delivery leaves pathway activity and cellular source unresolved.

In Piekos, the full Reactome BMP score was +0.126 (HC3 95% CI +0.020 to +0.231) in 25 severe PE+FGR versus 36 isolated FGR pregnancies. After restriction to the observed delivery-age overlap (32.71–38.29 weeks, 15 vs 16) and linear age adjustment, the estimate was +0.091 (−0.062 to +0.244). Only one isolated-FGR pregnancy delivered before 34 weeks, and age at PE onset is unavailable. This is directional context in a different cohort. The score uses 31 genes and log₂(median-ratio normalized count + 0.5). [Piekos data and code](https://github.com/spiekos/Hood-Lab-Placental-Multiomics)

The donor-paired comparison of decidua minus villi used 4 combined cases and 5 isolated FGR pregnancies from the inferred same sequencing round. The TGF-β pathway score difference between tissues was close to zero (+0.003 log₂ units, exact permutation p=0.992). None of the 50 paired Hallmark tests met contrast-wise FDR 0.05 in the three pilot comparisons. The sample size limits resolution of tissue differences. ER-to-Golgi gene set ranks were positively enriched in the restricted local and external comparisons. The fixed per-pregnancy score was close to zero in the restricted local fit and negative in the full local contrast, so it ranks below TGF-β for follow-up.

Ackerman showed the pairwise differential expression results for both tissues and enriched tissue-specific coexpression modules. Awamleh compared each affected group with preterm controls. Piekos analyzed pairwise placental expression for an interomics network containing known markers of placental injury. Aisagbonhi found TGF-β enrichment against a normal-size normotensive reference in both the small-for-gestational-age (SGA) PE and normotensive SGA groups. Yi reported TGF-β/BMP changes in EOPE versus controls, including increased BMPR2 and decreased BMP ligands. The narrower follow-up is to estimate the additional difference associated with EOPE in pregnancies already classified as FGR and assess whether specific components of the pathway recur. [Ackerman et al.](https://doi.org/10.1161/HYPERTENSIONAHA.122.20807) · [Awamleh et al.](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12920-019-0548-x) · [Piekos et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-025-08631-6) · [Aisagbonhi et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13098728/#S12) · [Yi et al.](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2021.768669)

The rank analysis uses signed Wald statistics from the tissue-specific DESeq2 fit (`~ fetal_sex + group`), an age-restricted villous refit of the same model, and the Awamleh group-only PyDESeq2 fit. Genes have a finite Wald test, base mean ≥10, and a unique HGNC mapping. `fgseaMultilevel` used MSigDB v2024.1.Hs and Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment across the 50 Hallmarks within each contrast. The separate ER–Golgi check uses three overlapping Reactome gene sets on a shared gene universe. Donor scores are unweighted means of log₂(median-ratio normalized count + 1) across genes with at least 10 raw reads in at least five samples in each villous cohort. The paired pilot normalizes each tissue separately and retains genes with 10 raw reads in at least four samples in each tissue. Its permutation p values are descriptive and assume exchangeable labels within the selected cohorts. This was an exploratory screen. The age and leave-one-out checks reuse donors.
