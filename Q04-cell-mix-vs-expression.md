# Q4 · Does preeclampsia change the cell mix, expression within a class, or both?

**Working hypothesis.** Early-onset preeclampsia (EOPE) may shift both recovered cell fractions and RNA expression within a cell class. Prioritize `FLT1` and `PGF` in syncytiotrophoblast-labelled cells, then test whether Hofbauer inflammatory expression rises even when the class’s recovered share falls. Campbell’s deconvolution results support a contribution from composition to some bulk associations. Admati’s source study provides the within-class hypotheses. [Campbell et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-023-04623-6) · [Admati et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37572658/)

## Analysis

1. Compare `PE_early` (10) with `control_early` (3) in Admati. Build a donor-by-class table from `n_cells` and divide by each donor’s total recovered cells. Include zeros for absent classes. Plot one dot per donor and report percentage-point differences and leave-one-control-out sensitivity. The source chat’s descriptive medians were 2.90% in controls and 4.08% in cases for syncytiotrophoblast, 11.66% and 6.45% for Hofbauer cells, and 7.64% and 13.97% for endothelium. These fractions depend on recovery and sampling. The syncytiotrophoblast’s multinucleated layer is especially difficult to represent by dissociation. [Pseudobulk profiles](openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/data/extension/pseudobulk/track01_pseudobulk_metadata.csv) · [Donor totals](openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/data/extension/pseudobulk/track01_pseudobulk_donors.csv)
2. Model raw summed RNA counts within each class with one profile per donor. Start at ≥20 captured cells and check ≥10 and ≥30. A zero-cell class contributes to the fraction plot. Its RNA expression is undefined. Estimate SCT `FLT1` and `PGF`, a prespecified Hofbauer inflammatory score, and secondary endothelial stress effects. Use EVT capture descriptively because only one early control passes the ≥20-cell floor. Show donor expression, intervals, and genome-wide FDR where relevant.
3. Leave out each early control in turn and inspect the four early PE donors without FGR as a descriptive check. Nine of ten cases delivered by cesarean and all three controls delivered vaginally. Show delivery age, mode, and FGR next to donor values. Within-class differences may reflect expression changes within subtypes or a shift in the subtype mix.
4. Fit the corresponding bulk villous contrast in Ackerman using EOPE without FGR (8) versus idiopathic preterm birth (5), with a 5-versus-5 age-overlap sensitivity. Compare candidate effects with the directions of SCT markers such as `CGA`, `CGB`, `ERVW-1`, and `PSG1` where measured. Log at least three Zeisel lookups. Ackerman and Admati are different cohorts, so the Admati fractions give context for the bulk signal at the cohort level. [Organizer workflow](openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/3-workflow.md) · [Marker matrix](openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/data/interpretation/track01_marker_matrix.csv)

## Figures and follow-up

Pair a recovered-fraction figure with a within-class expression figure and corresponding bulk effects. If fraction and identity markers shift while within-class estimates are small and precise, composition is plausible. If within-class SCT effects survive, a class-average expression shift is supported. If Hofbauer expression and fraction move in opposite directions, the two effects could partly offset in tissue RNA. Measure SCT coverage and RNA per unit of the layer in the same tissue sections from independent age- and delivery-context-comparable donors to distinguish those explanations.
