# Q3 · Which placental cell classes show the largest expression differences?

**Working hypothesis.** Early-onset preeclampsia may produce a clear `FLT1`-higher, `PGF`-lower pattern in syncytiotrophoblast (SCT), with stress and inflammatory programs in vascular, stromal, and myeloid cells. Use late PE versus late controls as the primary ranking because donor coverage is more balanced. Prespecify early PE versus early controls as exploratory. The Admati study motivates both the SCT benchmark and a look at extravillous trophoblasts (EVTs). [Admati et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37572658/)

## Cohort and analysis

1. Use the Admati extension, which contains 362 donor-by-cell-class profiles from 26 pregnancies across 14 classes. Audit unique donor-class IDs, alignment, library sizes, and captured cells. At a proposed ≥10-cell threshold, SCT, villous cytotrophoblast (VCT), endothelium, stroma, and Hofbauer cells retain 7 late cases and 6 late controls and 10 early cases and 3 early controls. Macrophages retain 9 early cases and 3 early controls. EVT retains just 2 late and 1 early control, so show EVT descriptively. Use ≥20 cells as a sensitivity check. [Profiles](openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/data/extension/pseudobulk/track01_pseudobulk_metadata.csv) · [Donors](openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/data/extension/pseudobulk/track01_pseudobulk_donors.csv)
2. Fit separate raw-count DESeq2 models by class and onset stratum with `~ pe_status`. Use one profile per donor as the replicate. Plot within-class PCA and normalized donor expression. For late PE, check `~ ga_delivery_weeks + pe_status` if estimable and compare rankings on the same donor set where feasible. For early PE, leave out each control in turn. Nine of ten early cases delivered by cesarean, all three controls delivered vaginally, and six cases had FGR compared with zero controls.
3. Define the genome-wide ranking as the 90th percentile of absolute shrunken log₂ fold changes across a common expressed-gene set. Report how many genes and donors enter each ranking. Pair it with signed pathway effects from each class’s own testable genes. The prespecified panel is SCT `FLT1` and `PGF`, endothelial and stromal angiogenesis and stress, VCT differentiation or proliferation, and inflammation in macrophages and Hofbauer cells assessed separately. Inspect `KDR`, `VEGFA`, `SPP1`, and `ISG15` where appropriate.
4. Check whether the leading class changes with the cell-count filter or donor set. Plot captured-cell fractions as context and flag pooled subtypes that could shift the broad-class mean. Record browser localization for at least three result genes. The browser and pseudobulk trace to Admati. Compare an early signal with Ackerman villous bulk as a separate tissue-level direction check. Use a donor-coverage figure, signed class-by-pathway heatmaps for each onset contrast, and donor plots for the lead result. A large early estimate and an imprecise late estimate call for the direct comparison in [Q7](Q07-early-vs-late-onset.md).
