# Which placental cell types carry the EOPE glycolytic RNA signature?

**Research question.** At delivery, which placental cell types show an early-onset preeclampsia (EOPE) increase in both Ackerman mRNA ratios, `LDHA/LDHB` and `PDK1/GOT1`? Is the pattern shared across lineages, concentrated in a few populations, or related to changes in the recovered cell mix? Ackerman used both ratios to summarize a hypoxia-linked program in a subset of EOPE placentas. Hofbauer cells and syncytiotrophoblast (SCT) enter the same screen as every other population. [Ackerman et al., Figure 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10192030/) · [Admati et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37572658/)

## 2. Calculate the ratios and run the same screen for every label

For each donor × cell type, sum the raw UMI for each gene. On those sums, compute `LDHA / LDHB` and `PDK1 / GOT1`, then take log₂ of each positive ratio. Use `PDK1` for pyruvate dehydrogenase kinase 1. A ratio with denominator zero is undefined. With a zero numerator and positive denominator, store the raw zero and omit its log₂ value in the initial screen. Keep the four counts and the exclusion reason in the results table. A common library-size scaling factor cancels within each ratio. For plots of individual genes, normalize each gene by the donor–type library size. [Ackerman et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10192030/) · [Pseudobulk benchmarking study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-25960-2)

Use the side chat's exploratory screen. Require **≥20 cells and ≥1 summed UMI for each of the four genes per donor**, and **≥3 eligible donors per group** for each type. Use the same eligible donors for both ratios within each type. Estimate the mean of the donor log₂ ratios in EOPE minus the mean in controls. Weight each donor equally and report the two effects, donor counts and raw expression alongside them. Display all 46 labels in the results, with the reason for low coverage where applicable. Donor subsets can differ among types, so a rank based on the initial means alone can reflect who was retained.

Repeat the screen with **≥50 cells** and, separately, with **≥5 UMI per gene**. For each threshold, record which labels remain eligible and recompute each eligible effect after dropping each control individually. Also inspect one-case-at-a-time effects, delivery-age overlap, the four EOPE donors without FGR, and a separate sensitivity with a documented 0.5 UMI offset for sparse ratios. Show exactly which donors a sensitivity retains. A positive pair of mean changes should be accompanied by a plot of both ratios for the same donors to see whether the two increases occur in the same pregnancies.

**Verified pilot.** Eleven of 46 labels pass the first threshold, and 10 have positive mean changes in both ratios. Both changes stay positive under the stricter thresholds and control omissions for Hofbauer, GALNT15 and LTN1 stromal cells, villous cytotrophoblast 2, proliferating villous cytotrophoblast, and arterial endothelium. M1-labeled macrophages have the largest observed pair (+2.28 and +1.64 log₂), with three eligible controls initially and two at the 50-cell threshold. At the organizer's broader resolution, eight of 14 classes pass the first threshold and seven have positive changes in both ratios. The whole-cell `TB_SCT` label has two eligible controls. The mature SCT nuclear estimates are +0.08 and +0.54 log₂ from three cases and two controls. The nuclear estimates describe SCT separately because the public nuclear dataset covers trophoblast and uses a different assay. [Verified donor ratios](../admati-glycolytic-ratios.csv) · [Admati et al., Figure 2](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666634023002283)

## 3. Test whether the effect differs between cell types

For a direct check within Admati, take the difference between two cell types’ log₂ ratios in each donor who has both types under the same QC rule. Compare that within-donor difference between cases and controls for each ratio. For example, a positive EOPE–control change in `Hofbauer − SCT` would favor a larger Hofbauer response. Use this as a secondary comparison and report how many donors are shared. Across all types, report estimates and sensitivity ranges as exploratory given the three controls, the delivery-mode imbalance and the fact that the thresholds have already been used in a pilot.

For replication, first audit a cohort such as **GSE319236** for villous sampling, EOPE definition, donor labels, delivery-age and labor/mode overlap, and quantifiable coverage of both fetal Hofbauer and mature SCT in a common assay. Apply the frozen screen and keep the discovery and replication cohorts distinct. In a cohort with adequate overlap, fit each ratio with `EOPE × cell_type`, a donor random intercept and supported covariates, or use matched donor contrasts when the design supplies matched controls. Report effect sizes and intervals, test the interaction directly and control multiplicity across follow-up contrasts. To call both ratios increased in a type, use the larger of their two one-sided p-values and apply Holm correction across eligible types. Require a replicated interaction to claim a preferential response. [GSE319236](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE319236) · [Sanchez-Corrales et al.](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10231766/)

## 4. Work out what could contribute to the tissue-level signal

Plot both ratios per Ackerman villous sample, starting with EOPE without FGR (8) versus idiopathic preterm birth (iPTB, 5). Show EOPE with FGR (7) as a separate group, a delivery-age-overlap view, and all four gene abundances. Ask whether both ratios are elevated together in a subset of pregnancies. Compare the direction and heterogeneity of within-cohort changes between bulk RNA-seq and Admati whole-cell UMI data. [Ackerman bulk source](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE203507) · [Ackerman et al., Figure 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10192030/)

Within Admati, reconstruct the ratio from the sum of the numerator and denominator counts across the captured classes. For a composition sensitivity, calculate each class’s gene UMI divided by its total UMI and its share of the donor’s total recovered UMI. Sum the products of those two quantities to reconstruct gene abundance, then take the ratio. Build control and EOPE reference profiles using means within each group. First change expression profiles from control to EOPE with control RNA shares fixed. Then change RNA shares from control to EOPE with control expression fixed. Compare both with changing expression and shares together. Show any classes that lack enough coverage to estimate a control profile, and repeat with captured-cell shares as a sensitivity. These weights reflect the assay’s recovery and RNA content. In particular, the mature syncytium is poorly represented in whole-cell data. [Placental composition study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-023-04623-6) · [Admati et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37572658/)

## 5. Move from RNA localization to mechanism

Check whether each increase is driven by its numerator, its denominator, or both. A `PDK1/GOT1` increase driven by falling `GOT1` provides weaker evidence for PDK1-mediated PDH inhibition. As an orthogonal RNA check, compare a fixed hypoxia or glycolysis gene set after removing the four ratio genes. Record browser lookups for all four genes and use cell markers to locate the candidate populations. The browser uses the Admati cohort and adds expression context. [Zeisel browser guide](../../openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/data/interpretation/zeisel_browser_guide.md) · [HIF–PDK1 study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16517405/)

In a follow-up with appropriately matched placentas, use multiplex RNA in situ or protein staining to verify the implicated population in villous tissue. Then test whether glucose-derived ¹³C reaches lactate and TCA intermediates differently in that population. To distinguish an adaptive response from a potential amplifier, hold the stress exposure constant while changing the implicated pathway and track cell viability plus a prespecified cell-function readout. Examples include inflammatory signaling for a macrophage result, angiogenic secretion for an SCT result, or barrier function for a vascular result. If PDK1 drives the candidate signal, measure PDH phosphorylation alongside flux. These experiments address function under controlled conditions. Establishing when the change appears relative to human disease onset requires temporal evidence. [HIF–PDK1 study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16517405/) · [Trophoblast metabolic reprogramming study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9890534/)
