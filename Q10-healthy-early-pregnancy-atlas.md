# Q10 · What does healthy early pregnancy tell us about PE signals at delivery?

**Working hypothesis.** A delivery-stage PE signature may include a program normally used by extravillous trophoblasts (EVTs) at the implantation site, a broader stress response in villi, or both. Compare one prespecified EVT differentiation or vascular-remodeling program with a prespecified hypoxia or glycolysis program. Villi sample fetal exchange tissue. Decidua basalis samples the maternal implantation site, where fetal EVTs encounter maternal stromal, immune, and arterial cells. The healthy maps supply normal cell states, tissue niches, and developmental timing. [Vento-Tormo et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-018-0698-6) · [Arutyunyan et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-05869-0) · [Greenbaum et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06298-9) · [Wang et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10316-x)

## Analysis

1. Start in GSE203507 villi with EOPE without FGR versus idiopathic preterm birth in the shared 29.857–33.000-week window (5 vs. 5). Use the full 8-versus-5 contrast as a sensitivity analysis. Plot age, inspect PCA and library QC, and check influential donors. Fit raw-count DESeq2 or PyDESeq2, keeping fold changes, intervals, and adjusted p-values. Preterm controls delivered spontaneously, which still leaves delivery-context differences. [Villous metadata](openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/data/track01_metadata_vt.csv) · [Organizer workflow](openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/3-workflow.md)
2. Freeze both gene programs before reviewing effects. Inspect `HLA-G`, `ITGA1`, and `MMP2` as EVT candidates and `ITGB6` and `AOC1` as examples of different EVT states. Keep broad identity markers separate from state markers. `NCAM1` can occur in vessel-associated EVT and decidual NK populations. For villous stress, use a fixed hypoxia or glycolysis set and inspect `LDHA`, `LDHB`, `PDK1`, and `GOT1` by donor. Ackerman’s metabolic pattern is a same-cohort benchmark. [Marker matrix](openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/data/interpretation/track01_marker_matrix.csv) · [Ackerman et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10192030/)
3. Annotate each candidate’s healthy cell state, maternal or fetal origin, tissue location, developmental window, and donor support. Start with the first-trimester HCA map and use the spatial maps to locate EVT states near maternal vessels. Check Wang’s across-gestation data for persistence and stage specificity. If quantifying gene overlap, use a shared measurable gene universe and report program coverage. Analyze each atlas on its own expression scale. [HCA fetal–maternal interface](https://explore.data.humancellatlas.org/projects/f83165c5-e2ea-4d15-a5cf-33f3550bffde)
4. Fit the same comparison separately in the decidua extension, which covers 27 of 30 pregnancies and all ten primary donors. The three missing decidua samples are all EOPE+FGR. Compare decidua and villous effects on the same donors. Use a paired tissue-by-disease test for a formal claim that the effect differs by tissue. Treat an isolated-FGR comparison as exploratory where age support allows. [Decidua metadata](openbio-datathon-2026/track-01/data/extension/decidua/track01_metadata_db.csv)
5. Log three Zeisel lookups for a robust villous hit, a developmental candidate, and a gene that helps distinguish identity from state. Use Admati pseudobulk selectively, keeping the donor as the replicate and reporting coverage. The early comparison starts at 10 cases and 3 controls. ## Figures and follow-up

Show sample structure and age overlap, donor-level effects for both programs, and an atlas-backed cell-state and tissue panel. A robust decidual EVT-state effect alongside villous stress would support compartment-specific associations at delivery. A weak EVT signal would leave the implantation-site hypothesis unresolved. Independent spatial samples could test EVT density and RNA per identified cell near maternal arteries. Testing whether a signature precedes PE requires early samples linked to later outcomes.
